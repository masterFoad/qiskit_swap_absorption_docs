This adds a private `_AbsorbIntoSwaps` pass that moves a commuting two-qubit interaction next to a routing SWAP on the same physical qubit pair, so that the existing two-qubit synthesis can optimize the two as one block. It runs at the start of the built-in translation stages at optimization levels 2 and 3.

Routing can separate an interaction from a SWAP by operations that commute with it, which hides the opportunity for joint synthesis. In the issue's example, moving the CP next to the SWAP saves one CX gate; in the simplest case, a SWAP followed by a CX on the same ordered pair needs two CX gates instead of four. The pass only reorders operations; the gate reduction comes from the subsequent synthesis.

### Validation

I ran 11 ten-qubit circuits through the full preset pipeline with absorption on and off, across six seeds and levels 2 and 3. In all 132 cases, both outputs match the original unitary, with layout and global phase taken into account.
